Johto Gold Mod

Run your own Pokémon Gold ROM locally in your browser. Optionally apply the project's IPS modification before starting the emulator. Your ROM is not uploaded by this page.

Select a modification to see what it changes.
Choose an IPS produced by the matching build. An IPS can corrupt an incompatible ROM revision; this page checks the original ROM's SHA-1 when a patch is selected.

Choose a ROM to begin.

Keyboard: click the game, then hold Space for 2× speed. Release to return to normal.

Emulation is provided by EmulatorJS, loaded from its official CDN. The emulator is not bundled here and initially requires internet. Save data may be stored by the emulator in your browser; do not rely on it without backups. The ROM is read locally, and never sent to our server by this page.
